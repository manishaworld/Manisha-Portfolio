Biography

From competitive athletics in Nepal to Software Engineering in South Korea—my journey is shaped by discipline, resilience, and a drive to solve technical challenges.

Foundations in Kathmandu

Born and raised in Kathmandu, Nepal, I developed a strong academic curiosity early on. While naturally drawn to the sciences, I quickly learned the value of persistent effort when working through complex mathematical concepts—transforming what began as a challenge into one of my most dependable analytical strengths.

Alongside my primary studies, creative writing and traditional performing arts gave me a balanced outlet, instilling a structured appreciation for rhythm, expression, and cultural heritage.

Athletics & Competitive Discipline

Athletics played a pivotal role in shaping my work ethic. For four years, I competed as an active football player in regional futsal circuits and represented my district in competitive marathons. Consistently placing among the top runners earned me official recognition and awards presented by the Ministry of Youth and Sports.

To focus on technical academics, I channeled that same athletic endurance into the Sciences (+2) at Birendra Secondary School. Graduating with academic honors set the trajectory for my undergraduate journey in engineering.

Engineering & University Life in South Korea

Relocating to South Korea marked the start of my undergraduate degree in Software Engineering. Immersing myself in a new language and culture tested my adaptability, while building real-world software sharpened my technical foundation.

Applying theoretical coursework under time constraints led to winning 2nd Prize in the University Coding Competition for two consecutive years. Beyond code, navigating international student life has fostered strong cross-cultural collaboration skills and lasting technical partnerships.

Beyond the Terminal

Physical endurance and cultural performance remain central to how I recharge and maintain mental focus.

Cultural Dance

Traditional Nepali dance keeps me grounded in my roots. Preparing group choreography requires spatial awareness, coordination, and team synchronization that mirror collaborative group work.

Football & Futsal

Quick decision-making under defensive pressure, mutual accountability, and situational awareness are direct lessons taken from years on the pitch.

Long-Distance Running

Distance running builds sustained mental stamina. Long training cycles and competitive podium finishes taught me to manage pacing, conserve energy, and execute deliberately toward long-term goals.
